Panel Generator is free to use for anything that isn't commercial. It is not open source.

## What you can do

- **Use it** for hobby projects, personal study, teaching, research, and non-profit organisations.
- **Change it** for your own non-commercial use, and share your changes non-commercially. Keep the licence and its `Required Notice:` lines with any copy you pass on.
- **Sell the panels you make**, and the synths, kits and modules they're for. See the extra permission below.

## What you can't do without a commercial licence

- Sell the software, or a changed version of it, or charge for copies of it.
- Put it in something you sell or charge for: a paid app or plugin, a hosted or online version, or a service that designs panels for other people for money.

## Extra permission: you can sell the panels you make

You may use Panel Generator to design panels for your own products, and **sell those panels**, alone or as part of a kit, module or instrument, even as a business. The panels, PCB files, gerbers, pictures and other files you make with it are yours. That includes the footprints from `PanelParts.pretty` and `Eurorack.pretty` placed in your panels. The licensor gives this permission in addition to the PolyForm Noncommercial License.

In short: making and selling your own synths, kits and panels is fine. Selling or charging for the tool itself, or for using it on other people's designs, needs a commercial licence.

## Commercial licence

To use Panel Generator commercially, ask for a commercial licence. Open an issue on this repository titled **"Commercial licence"**, or contact lonesoulsurfer through [GitHub](https://github.com/lonesoulsurfer). Say what you would like to use it for.

## Earlier versions

Versions **0.26.0 and earlier** were released under the MIT licence. Copies of those versions stay under MIT. Everything from 0.27.0 on is under the licence above.
